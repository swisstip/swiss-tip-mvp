# mvp-poland source inventory

**Last update:** 27 September 2026

The exact official pages of the civic-participation topics: the participatory budget, the local initiative and grants for non-governmental organisations, and consultations and the resident's voice in Kraków, Warsaw and Katowice, with the national acts that set the common rules. Every page below was opened by the research of 27 September 2026 and confirmed by an independent verifier; the downloader snapshots exactly these URLs and follows no link. Pages marked *not fetched* are on hosts behind a bot challenge that the crawler cannot pass (see the registry notes in `sources.json`).

The national acts are cited through the Sejm's ELI API, which serves the same PDFs as Dziennik Ustaw; dziennikustaw.gov.pl and ISAP disallow every crawler in robots.txt. City council resolutions are cited as published in the voivodeship's official journal where the research found them there, and otherwise from the city's Public Information Bulletin.
