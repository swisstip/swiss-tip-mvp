# Wallisellen MVP source inventory

**Last update:** 16 September 2026

The MVP snapshots the official German pages below from Stadt Wallisellen. The
selection covers durable resident-facing topics and excludes individual news,
events, vacancies, people, businesses and downloadable legal or administrative
files, except the five waste documents linked from the waste disposal page. A linked directory is evidence of the directory and its purpose, not of
every item that may appear in it later.
